# Problem, Stakeholder Analysis and Business Values

## Problem

The current business system contains numerous issues that our stakeholders have
experienced. These issues include staff facing difficulty prioritizing tasks and
coordinating work, lack of security/privacy of request information, reporting has been
found to be difficult to audit, requesters have reported cases of uncertainty about the
status of their request, and many more system issues that make the system difficult for
our stakeholders to use. These problems may lead to project failure due to the business
failing to address stakeholder concerns (Li, Ng and Skitmore, 2013).

## Stakeholder Analysis

Key members of the community who will be using the system and will be affected/will
affect, business outcomes are considered our important stakeholders.

| Stakeholder | Role | Needs | Influence on CivicConnect | Possible influence by CivicConnect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Requester/Users | Sending in requests | - Easy use of requesting system.<br>- Visibility of request status. | Low/Medium | High |
| Staff | Handling requests | - Convenient handling and prioritisation of requests.<br>- Improved coordination of work. | High | High |
| Management | Overview of CivicConnect processes | - Improved insights of business performance | High | High |
| System Administrator | Maintaining system and security | - Security, reliability, access control. | High | Low/Medium |

## Business Values

CivicConnect provides a controlled system establishing a reliable and traceable way to
submit, manage and monitor service requests. This reduces operational and administrative
burden while improving visibility amongst requesters/users and accountability among staff.
